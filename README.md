# ❤️ Heart Disease Analysis Dashboard - Power BI Project

This Power BI dashboard presents a detailed analysis of **heart disease survival patterns** based on **age**, **gender**, and key medical indicators such as **ejection fraction**, **serum creatinine**, **high blood pressure**, **diabetes**, and more.

It helps answer critical questions about who is most at risk and what patterns exist among survivors and non-survivors.

---

## 🔍 Project Summary

Heart disease is one of the leading causes of death globally. With data visualization, we can uncover patterns that help support early detection, better healthcare decisions, and awareness of personal health risks.

This dashboard was created using a heart disease dataset to visually explore:
- Survival trends by **age group** and **gender**
- Key indicators linked to higher mortality
- Impact of medical conditions like diabetes, anemia, and smoking

---

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

### ✅ Overall Stats:
- 🟢 **Survival Rate:** 68.04%
- 🧓 **Average Age of Survivors:** 58.83 years
- ✅ **Total Survivors:** 132
- ❌ **Total Deaths:** 62

### ❓ Key Questions Answered:
1. **Who survived and who didn’t?**
2. **How does survival vary by age and gender?**
3. **Do low ejection fraction or high serum creatinine increase death risk?**
4. **What comorbidities are common in high-risk patients?**
5. **What can we learn to prevent future cases?**

---

## 📌 Observations

- 🔻 **Survival rate decreases** significantly in patients aged 71+ and below 40.
- 🩺 **Low ejection fraction** and **high serum creatinine** are common in non-survivors.
- ⚠️ **Diabetes**, **high blood pressure**, and **anemia** are major risk factors.
- 🚹 Males showed slightly better survival in middle-age groups compared to females.
- 👩‍🦰 Female patients are fewer in number, and their survival rate drops in older age.
